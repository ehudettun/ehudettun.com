Double bass lessons with Ehud Ettun

Ehud Ettun teaches private double bass and jazz bass lessons in person in the Boston area and online for students anywhere in the world. He teaches jazz at NEC Prep (New England Conservatory’s precollege and continuing-education program), is a graduate of New England Conservatory, and founded Internal Compass Music, a music college and jazz hub in Mitzpe Ramon, Israel. To ask about lessons, email ehudettun@gmail.com.

Who the lessons are for

  • Upright/double bass players, and electric bassists moving to upright.
  • Young players and adults; private students as well as ensembles.
  • Composers and other instrumentalists who want to work on jazz language and arranging.

What students work on

  • Sound, intonation and technique on the double bass, pizzicato and arco.
  • Walking bass lines, time feel and playing in a rhythm section.
  • Jazz standards, harmony, improvisation and soloing on the bass.
  • Repertoire beyond standards — classical pieces, traditional and Israeli songs — and writing original music.

In person or online

In-person lessons take place in the Boston area. Online lessons are available worldwide by video call. Ensembles and schools can also ask about masterclasses and clinics.

How to book a lesson

Email ehudettun@gmail.com with your level, goals, location or time zone, and whether you prefer in-person or online lessons.
