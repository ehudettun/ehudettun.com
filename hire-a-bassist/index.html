Sideman · Session · Recording

Looking for a double bass player?

Ehud Ettun is a Boston-based jazz double bassist (upright bass) available as a sideman for live gigs, and for studio sessions and recordings. He has performed and recorded with Kenny Werner, Anat Cohen and George Garzone, and plays regularly on the Boston jazz scene. Email ehudettun@gmail.com with the date, location and music.

What he plays

  • Jazz — standards, straight-ahead, and contemporary/original music.
  • Jewish and Israeli music and traditional songs from around the world.
  • Classical-influenced and arco playing — for example his all-bass arrangement of Mozart’s Divertimento in D Major on YouTube.

As a sideman

Ettun has performed and recorded with Kenny Werner, Anat Cohen and George Garzone. Recent Boston appearances include the Tim Ray Trio featuring Edmar Colón at Scullers Jazz Club, the Sam Spear Group at UnCommon Stage, and the Nick Brust Group at Mad Monkfish in Cambridge.

Sessions and recordings

He has made six albums as leader or co-leader (from Heading North, 2012, to Knights of Silence, 2024). For a remote recording, describe the project and delivery format in your email.

How to hire

Send the date, location, type of gig or session, the music (or a link), and your budget to ehudettun@gmail.com.
