Booking Ehud Ettun for your festival or series

Ehud Ettun is an Israeli-born, Boston-based double bassist and composer whose contemporary jazz blends the jazz tradition with classical music and traditional Jewish and Israeli melodies. He leads the Ehud Ettun Trio, co-leads the piano–bass duo The Yabuno Ettun Project with Japanese pianist Haruka Yabuno, and has performed at the Blue Note (New York) and the Kennedy Center (Washington, D.C.). To book, email ehudettun@gmail.com.

The sound

A melodic, vocal approach to the upright bass, rooted in Giovanni Bottesini, Charles Mingus, Charlie Haden and Ray Brown, with influences from Chick Corea, Carla Bley and Brad Mehldau. Programs mix original compositions with arrangements of jazz standards and traditional songs from around the world — such as ‘Arirang’ and ‘Alfonsina y el Mar’ — and from Israel. The Boston Globe wrote that “Ettun can conjure the deep soulfulness of Charlie Haden.”

Ensembles available

  • Ehud Ettun Trio — original compositions and arrangements; latest album Deep in the Mountains.
  • The Yabuno Ettun Project (piano–bass duo with Haruka Yabuno) — fifteen years together; originals, classical pieces and standards; albums BiPolar and Knights of Silence. Well suited to concert halls, chamber series and intimate rooms.
  • Internal Compass Collective — an international ensemble born out of Internal Compass Music, the music college and jazz hub Ettun founded in Mitzpe Ramon, in Israel’s Negev desert.

Performance history

Blue Note (New York) and the Kennedy Center (Washington, D.C.); international touring with stops in Boston, Toronto, Berlin, Warsaw, Tokyo and Seoul. As a sideman he has performed and recorded with Kenny Werner, Anat Cohen and George Garzone. In Boston he appears at clubs including Scullers Jazz Club and Mad Monkfish.
